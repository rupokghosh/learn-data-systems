Describing load: the timeline problem

"Will it scale?" means nothing until you say which load is growing. The first step is to describe today's load with a few numbers, called load parameters.

Pick the numbers that matter

Which load parameters matter depends on the system: requests per second for a web server, the ratio of reads to writes for a database, the number of people online at once for a chat app, or the hit rate of a cache. Sometimes the average case is what hurts. Often it is a handful of extreme cases.
